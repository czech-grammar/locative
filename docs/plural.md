# Locative Plural Endings

Good news! The Locative plural is **much simpler** than the singular. All genders share the same basic endings.

## Quick Reference

![Locative Plural Cheatsheet](/img/locative-plural-cheatsheet.svg)

---

## The "-ch Club"

:::tip The Golden Rule
Almost all Locative plurals end in **-ch** (specifically **-ách**, **-ích**, or **-ech**).

Welcome to the "-ch club"!
:::

---

## Masculine Plural

### Hard Masculine → -ech / -ích

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Nominative Pl. | Locative Pl. |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------------|:-------------|
| Hard animate | **-ech** | studenti | studenti | o student**ech** |
| Hard animate | **-ech** | bratři | bratři | o bratr**ech** |
| Hard inanimate | **-ech** | hrady | hrady | na hrad**ech** |
| Hard inanimate | **-ech** | stoly | stoly | na stol**ech** |
| Hard inanimate | **-ech** | domy | domy | v dom**ech** |

### Soft Masculine → -ích

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Nominative Pl. | Locative Pl. |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------------|:-------------|
| Soft animate | **-ích** | muži | muži | o muž**ích** |
| Soft animate | **-ích** | učitelé | učitelé | o učitel**ích** |
| Soft inanimate | **-ích** | stroje | stroje | ve stroj**ích** |
| Soft inanimate | **-ích** | pokoje | pokoje | v pokoj**ích** |

## Pattern Summary

| Gender | Hard Pattern | Soft Pattern |
|:-------|:-------------|:-------------|
| **Masculine** | -ech | -ích |
| **Feminine (-a)** | -ách | — |
| **Feminine (-e)** | — | -ích |
| **Feminine (cons.)** | -ech | -ích |
| **Neuter** | -ech / -ách | -ích |

## Practice: Plural Endings

Put the nouns in the Locative plural:

1. v ___ (město) → *in the cities*
2. o ___ (student) → *about the students*
3. na ___ (hrad) → *at the castles*
4. ve ___ (škola) → *in the schools*
5. v ___ (restaurace) → *in the restaurants*
6. o ___ (žena) → *about the women*
7. v ___ (pokoj) → *in the rooms*
8. na ___ (ulice) → *on the streets*
9. na ___ (nádraží) → *at the stations*
10. v ___ (les) → *in the forests*

<details>
<summary>**Check Answers**</summary>

1. ve **městech** (neuter hard → -ech)
2. o **studentech** (masc. hard → -ech)
3. na **hradech** (masc. hard → -ech)
4. ve **školách** (fem. hard → -ách)
5. v **restauracích** (fem. soft → -ích)
6. o **ženách** (fem. hard → -ách)
7. v **pokojích** (masc. soft → -ích)
8. na **ulicích** (fem. soft → -ích)
9. na **nádražích** (neuter -í → -ích)
10. v **lesech** (masc. hard → -ech)

</details>
