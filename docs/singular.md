# Locative Singular Endings

The Locative singular has different endings based on **gender** and whether the noun follows a **hard** or **soft** pattern.

## Quick Reference

![Locative Singular Cheatsheet](/img/locative-singular-cheatsheet.svg)

---

## Masculine Nouns

### Animate Masculine (people, animals)

The most common ending is **-ovi**, but **-u** and **-i** are also used.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| Hard (-ovi) | **-ovi** | bratr (brother) | o bratr**ovi** |
| Hard (-ovi) | **-ovi** | student | o student**ovi** |
| Hard (-ovi) | **-ovi** | Petr | o Petr**ovi** |
| Soft (-i/-ovi) | **-i** or **-ovi** | muž (man) | o muž**i** / muž**ovi** |
| Soft (-i/-ovi) | **-i** or **-ovi** | učitel (teacher) | o učitel**i** / učitel**ovi** |

:::tip When to use -i vs -ovi
For soft masculine animates, both endings work. **-ovi** sounds slightly more formal/written, **-i** is more casual/spoken.
:::

### Inanimate Masculine (things)

Most take **-ě** or **-e** (with palatalization) or **-u**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| Hard → -ě/-e | **-ě** / **-e** | hrad (castle) | na hrad**ě** |
| Hard → -ě/-e | **-e** | stůl (table) | na stol**e** |
| Hard → -ě/-e | **-e** | les (forest) | v les**e** |
| Hard → -u | **-u** | dům (house) | v dom**ě** / dom**u** |
| Soft → -i | **-i** | stroj (machine) | ve stroj**i** |
| Soft → -i | **-i** | pokoj (room) | v pokoj**i** |

:::warning Palatalization Alert!
Before **-ě**, consonants change:
- **k → c**: rok → ro**ce** (in the year)
- **h → z**: sníh → na sně**hu** (exception: -u form)
- **ch → š**: vzduch → ve vzdu**chu** (often -u form)
- **r → ř**: papír → na papí**ře**
:::

## Feminine Nouns

### Hard Feminine (-a nouns)

These change **-a** to **-ě** or **-e**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -a → -ě | **-ě** | žena (woman) | o žen**ě** |
| -a → -ě | **-ě** | škola (school) | ve škol**e** |
| -a → -e (palatal) | **-e** | Praha | v Praz**e** (h→z) |
| -a → -e (palatal) | **-e** | kniha (book) | v kniz**e** (h→z) |
| -a → -e (palatal) | **-e** | matka (mother) | o matc**e** (k→c) |
| -a → -e (palatal) | **-e** | noha (leg) | na noz**e** (h→z) |

### Soft Feminine (-e/-ě nouns)

These change to **-i**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -e → -i | **-i** | restaurace | v restaurac**i** |
| -e → -i | **-i** | ulice (street) | na ulic**i** |
| -e → -i | **-i** | kancelář (office) | v kancelář**i** |

### Consonant-ending Feminine

These also take **-i**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -∅ → -i | **-i** | píseň (song) | o písn**i** |
| -∅ → -i | **-i** | postel (bed) | na postel**i** |
| -∅ → -i | **-i** | věc (thing) | o věc**i** |
| -∅ → -i | **-i** | noc (night) | v noc**i** |

## Neuter Nouns

### Hard Neuter (-o nouns)

These change **-o** to **-ě** or **-u**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -o → -ě | **-ě** | město (city) | ve měst**ě** |
| -o → -ě | **-ě** | auto (car) | v aut**ě** |
| -o → -ě | **-ě** | kino (cinema) | v kin**ě** |
| -o → -u | **-u** | divadlo (theatre) | v divadle/**u** |

### Soft Neuter (-e/-ě nouns)

These change to **-i**.

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -e → -i | **-i** | moře (sea) | na moř**i** |
| -e → -i | **-i** | srdce (heart) | v srdc**i** |
| -ě → -i | **-i** | letiště (airport) | na letišt**i** |

### -í Neuter Nouns

These **don't change** in the Locative!

| Pattern | Ending | Example | Locative |
|:--------|:-------|:--------|:---------|
| -í → -í | (no change) | nádraží (station) | na nádraž**í** |
| -í → -í | (no change) | náměstí (square) | na náměst**í** |
| -í → -í | (no change) | pondělí (Monday) | v pondělí |

### Common Neuter Words

| Word | Meaning | Locative | Preposition |

| kino | cinema | v kině | v |

| okno | window | na okně | na |
| moře | sea | na moři | na |
| pole | field | na poli | na |

## Pattern Summary

| Gender | Hard Pattern | Soft Pattern |
|:-------|:-------------|:-------------|
| **Masc. Animate** | -ovi | -i / -ovi |
| **Masc. Inanimate** | -ě / -e / -u | -i |
| **Feminine** | -ě / -e | -i |
| **Neuter** | -ě / -u | -i |
| **Neuter -í** | (no change) | — |

<summary>**Check Answers**</summary>

5. o **knize** (h→z palatalization)
6. v **restauraci** (fem. soft → -i)
7. ve **městě** (neuter hard → -ě)
8. na **moři** (neuter soft → -i)
9. na **nádraží** (neuter -í → no change)
10. v **lese** (masc. inanimate → -e)
